# Audio credits

## Drums — `drums/`

Mixed down from the **DrumGizmo MuldjordKit** by Lars Muldjord (www.muldjord.com, www.drumgizmo.org),
SFZ port by kinwie: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/DrumGizmo.MuldjordKit

Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: selected hits of the kick (KdrumL), snare, closed and open hi-hat, china and left crash; the
multi-mic channels of each hit were mixed to stereo, trimmed, faded and level-normalized per
instrument. File names: `<drum>-a<n>` = loud hits (accents), `<drum>-n<n>` = normal hits.

## Guitar DI notes — `guitar/`

From **Electric Guitar FSBS (direct)** by the FreePats project,
https://github.com/freepats/electric-guitar-FSBS-direct — released under CC0 1.0 (public domain).
Direct (unamplified) recordings of a Fender electric guitar's bridge pickup.

Changes: 18 pitches (MIDI 36–85) × 2 takes of the louder layer; trimmed to start at the pick attack,
cut to 2.2 s (rhythm range) or 2.5 s (lead range) with a short fade, downmixed/resampled to 44.1 kHz
mono and converted to 16-bit FLAC. File names: `di-<midi>-<take>.flac`.

## Guitar cabinet impulse responses — `cab/`

From **Jester's Brutal Pack** by Jester Dyne Productions (Bastian Karschewski),
https://www.jester-dyne-productions.com/brutal-ir-pack/ — released under CC0 1.0 (public domain).

- `v30-sm57.flac` — patch 1: Celestion Vintage 30, Shure SM57.
- `v30-rockdriver-blend.flac` — patch 8: Celestion Rockdriver Jr. + Vintage 30, Sennheiser e606 + SM57.

Changes: 44.1 kHz versions, trimmed to 200 ms (room tail removed), converted to 16-bit FLAC.
